On the Intel NPU, the OpenVINO EP compiles at session load in 0.6 to 396 s (more than 10 s for 25 of 50 files), places no node on the CPU, and does not load 2 files (dewarp, yolact); per run, the as-is time divided by the precompiled one is 1.01× at the median and more than 1.2× in 6 of 50 (at most 1.33×). The blob loads in 0.003 to 0.18 s.
- **Precompiled slower:** the X2E's two DETR graph-B files ran 3.3× and 4.4× slower precompiled; on the Intel NPU they run the same both ways (RT-DETRv2 graph B 87.0 ms precompiled, 87.7 ms compiled at load; D-FINE graph B 96.9 and 97.5 ms). One file is far slower precompiled here: sam2_encoder, 1973 ms against 107 ms (18.5×); Windows' GPU Engine counters show both forms running on the NPU. The cause is not established.
